Maison Heritage

Born in Mongolia, Refined for the World

Our fibers are sourced from nomadic herding communities in Mongolia where centuries of craftsmanship meet the world’s finest natural cashmere. Each Maison Erdene silhouette is designed with restrained European tailoring and a quiet old-money sensibility—made to be worn for years, not seasons.
